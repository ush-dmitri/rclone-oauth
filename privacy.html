Privacy Policy

This application is a private rclone OAuth client. It is used only by its owner to copy personal photos from a local computer to the owner's own Google Drive.

It requests the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file scope and can access only the files and folders it creates. It does not read, collect, transmit, share, or sell any other personal data.

No analytics, advertising, or tracking are used. The data remains in the owner's Google Drive and on the owner's computer.

Contact: the owner of this application.
